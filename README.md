Entry for [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23) on make.fun — $2 to the worst, most useless app pitch, free to enter, blind rounds.

## The entry

One sentence pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable, at most 240 characters:

> Shelfie is the $4.99-a-month app that photographs your bookshelf, grades every spine by color harmony, and emails you a weekly report ranking the books your guests will assume you have actually read.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes that sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. The sealed scorer gates it: UTF-8 text, 1–240
characters, not only white space. A house judge then compares entries two
at a time on specificity, funniness, and how perfectly pointless the app is.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
